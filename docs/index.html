DuoBuddy

A friendly voice assistant for Android TV. Ask it to open apps, pause, change the volume, set timers or answer questions.

Download for Android TV How to install

Version 1.0.0 beta 2 · about 95 MB · Android TV and Google TV, Android 8 or later

On your TV, open the Downloader app and enter

eonurk.github.io/DuoBuddy

Then select Download for Android TV on this page.

Install on your TV

  1. Get Downloader

    Open the Google Play Store on your TV, search for Downloader (by AFTVnews) and install it. It is free.

  2. Open this page on the TV

    In Downloader, type eonurk.github.io/DuoBuddy and press Go. Select Download for Android TV.

  3. Allow the install

    If Android asks, allow Downloader to install unknown apps, then go back and select Install. You can delete the downloaded file afterwards.

  4. Open DuoBuddy

    Find DuoBuddy in your apps row. The first time, it guides you through connecting an AI provider.

Other ways: copy the file to a USB stick and open it with a file manager on the TV, or run adb install DuoBuddy-TV.apk from a computer.

Connect an AI provider

  1. Choose a model

    In DuoBuddy, open Model and pick OpenAI or Google Gemini, then Set up on phone.

  2. Paste your key on your phone

    With your phone on the same Wi-Fi, scan the QR code on the TV and paste your API key. You can also type it on the TV with Enter key on TV.

  3. Talk or type

    Choose Talk and allow the microphone, or Type. Try “Open YouTube”, “Volume up” or “Set a timer for 5 minutes”.

You use your own provider account, and AI replies may cost money there. Simple media, volume and timer commands work without a key.

Good to know

Privacy

  • Your key is encrypted on the TV with Android Keystore.
  • Requests go straight from the TV to the provider you chose.
  • Speech is generated on the TV and works offline.

Beta

  • Tested on a Xiaomi Mi Box 4. Other ARM TVs should work but are less tested.
  • No wake word yet. Use Talk or your remote’s assistant button.
  • How much it can control depends on the TV and each app.
