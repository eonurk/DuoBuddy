DuoBuddy embedded English speech

Voice: en_US-amy-low, 16 kHz, from rhasspy/piper-voices, packaged by sherpa-onnx.
The upstream MODEL_CARD and model configuration are included alongside the ONNX file.
https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/tree/main/en/en_US/amy/low
Dataset attribution: https://github.com/MycroftAI/mimic3-voices

Runtime: sherpa-onnx 1.13.8 (Apache-2.0), ONNX Runtime (MIT), Kotlin (Apache-2.0).
The publisher APK also includes AndroidX/Compose runtime classes (Apache-2.0).
Only runtime DEX, speech assets and native libraries are reused; the publisher's
manifest, app activities, services, resources and permissions are not installed.
https://github.com/k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx/tree/v1.13.8/android/SherpaOnnxTtsEngine
https://github.com/microsoft/onnxruntime
https://github.com/JetBrains/kotlin
https://android.googlesource.com/platform/frameworks/support/

Pronunciation: csukuangfj/piper-phonemize and eSpeak NG (GPL-3.0).
https://github.com/csukuangfj/piper-phonemize
https://github.com/espeak-ng/espeak-ng
