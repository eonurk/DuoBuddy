DuoBook TV Companion

1. Double-click Start Buddy.command if the controller is not already running. Leave its Terminal window open.
2. Open the local setup link printed in that Terminal window to choose a voice and use the controls.
3. Press and release the TV remote microphone button, wait for Listening, then speak.

Keep the controller Mac and M1-2 awake.
Project Files opens the existing live workspace.
